# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone who
participates in AIK, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, and code—not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials, private traces, or
  personal information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other material
that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

To report conduct privately, contact the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. If no private channel is available, open an issue containing only a request
for private contact—do not include sensitive details publicly. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the
project elsewhere.
